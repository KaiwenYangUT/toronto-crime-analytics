\section*{Code and data availability}
\small
The code, the pinned checksums of the input files, all result files and a companion website with interactive versions of most of the figures (built with Vega-Lite, \citealt{satyanarayan2017vegalite}) are in the project repository \textit{[link added at release]}. Every number in this paper is generated from the repository's result files. Incident data: Toronto Police Service Public Safety Data Portal, \url{https://data.tps.ca/}; contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence -- Ontario. Neighbourhood features: City of Toronto Neighbourhood Profiles, \url{https://open.toronto.ca/dataset/neighbourhood-profiles/}; contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence -- Toronto. This is an independent analysis and is not endorsed by the Toronto Police Service or the City of Toronto.
