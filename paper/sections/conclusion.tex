\section{Conclusion}
Answering the same narrow question by inference and by prediction gives complementary, not contradictory, results. The odds that a reported property-type incident is a robbery fell after 2019 and vary strongly by hour, yet the time features alone improve only modestly on the base rate; premises and location type, which partly define the crime types, supply most of the predictive lift, and census features add little. Reporting inference and prediction together, with intervals and out-of-sample designs, keeps each claim to what it supports.
