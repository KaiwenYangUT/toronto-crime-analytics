# Design review, pass 2 (P8)

Scope: 7 routes at 375 / 768 / 1280 in light and dark (fold + full page, 84 shots), Explore interactions,
keyboard focus, mobile menu, reduced motion, figure alternatives, paper PDF (6 pages).
Scripts: `reviews/shots.mjs`, `reviews/interact.mjs`, `reviews/targets.mjs`. Shots in `reviews/shots/`
(`<route>-<width>-<mode>-fold|full.png`). Measured: no horizontal page overflow on any route/width/mode;
no console errors; body 16 px; prose column 900 px max.

## Per page: 10-second test

- **Overview (/)**: PARTIAL FAIL. H1 says place beats time. At 1280x800 the fold holds three finding cards (0.55; 0.197 to 0.593; 0.242), so the 0.197 to 0.593 card does carry the H1. The first and left-most card is the post-2019 odds ratio, which the H1 does not mention. The only chart is the robbery-share-over-time line, below the fold at 1280 (starts ~y 830), and it shows a different finding (share fell from a fifth to a tenth). At 375 the fold shows only H1, subtitle, source chip and the start of card 1. A first-time reader takes away "something fell after 2019, and place matters", not one sentence. Evidence: home-1280-light-fold.png, home-1280-light-full.png, home-375-light-fold.png.
- **Explore (/explore)**: PASS at 1280 (H1, live count and map in the fold). FAIL at 375 and 768: map starts at y ~735 (375) and ~590 (768) so at 375 only the count card is visible; the Filters button is the only prompt. The H1 ("differ by place, weekday and hour, every filter changes the count") is a usage statement, not a finding; the user must interact to see it. Evidence: explore-375-light-fold.png, explore-768-dark-fold.png.
- **Explain (/explain)**: PASS at 1280 (H1 plus 0.55 card with CI and "does not mean" line next to the forest plot). PARTIAL at 375: the 0.55 card is far below the forest plot, whose first row ("Post-2019 period 0.55") is the only fold evidence for the H1; the chart's own title (hour of day) is a different message than the H1. Evidence: explain-1280-light-fold.png, explain-375-light-fold.png, explain-375-dark-full.png.
- **Predict (/predict)**: PASS at 1280 for the chart's visible part (filled dots at 0.5 to 0.6 vs low ones), but the rung labels L1..L5 are below the fold, so the viewer cannot tell which rung is which. FAIL at 375: fold is H1, a 3-line source pill, tabs and a 4-row legend; chart starts below the fold. Evidence: predict-1280-light-fold.png, predict-375-light-fold.png.
- **Paper (/paper)**: PARTIAL. H1 equals the paper's finding and the 'In brief' text restates it, but there is no figure or PDF preview above the fold; main visual is a button. Acceptable for a download page, but breaks the "main visual in fold" rule. Evidence: paper-1280-light-fold.png.
- **Methods (/methods)**: PASS as a reference page (H1 states a claim, table starts in fold). The in-page nav highlights the wrong item (see M-1).
- **About (/about)**: PASS (H1 is the statement; text only, appropriate).

## Checked and fine

- No horizontal overflow and no console errors on any of the 42 route x width x mode combinations (see `_metrics.txt`); exactly one H1 per page, each stating a finding.
- Body text 16 px; type scale consistent across pages; same header, card, callout, figure and button components on every page; consistent categorical colours (robbery vermillion, auto theft blue etc.) on Explore and in the paper.
- Dark mode: no regressions found. Map basemap, charts, tables, callouts, segmented controls and cards all re-themed (explore-1280-dark-fold.png, predict-1280-dark-full.png, menu-open-375-dark.png). Map clusters stay legible in dark.
- Focus visibility: every Tab stop tested on Overview (skip link, brand, all 7 nav links, theme toggle, source chip, cards, table/CSV buttons) and Explore (checkbox) has a 2 px solid accent outline; skip link is first and visible (kbd-home-tab1.png, kbd-explore-tab14.png). Mobile menu opens with all links and the theme toggle visible, light and dark (menu-open-375-*.png). Explore "Filters" opens a bottom sheet with a "Show results" button (explore-filters-sheet-375.png).
- Reduced motion: no active transitions or animations with prefers-reduced-motion (measured 0 elements).
- Every figure has "View data table" and CSV (and SVG where offered): Overview 1/1, Explore 4/4, Explain 2/2, Predict 3/3. No images lacking alt text.
- Explore interaction: enabling Auto theft changes counts from 25,242 to 55,765 records (live region updates); switching to "Near a destination" re-centres the map on Little Canada with a 300 m ring, labelled centre and updated count/charts.
- Copy: every page states reported incidents / not risk; "What this does not mean" lines present on finding cards; no risk, safety or causal wording found in the visible copy. (Explain H1 "was much less likely to be a robbery" is conditional on a reported incident; subtitle says association, not risk or cause.)
- Paper: Source Serif 4 body with Inter headings and figure text is applied consistently; figure colours match the site; Table 1 and Table 2 are readable, with rules and grouped sub-headings; captions fit the width; no orphaned headings; 6 pages; references complete; Fig. 3 legend (filled vs diamond) and baseline lines are clear.

## Verdict

0 blockers, 9 should-fixes (items 1 to 9), 11 nits.
