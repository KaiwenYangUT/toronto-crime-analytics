## Checked and fine

- **Overview.** 159,056 (n); the post-2019 OR 0.55 with the cluster-robust 0.52 to 0.58; PR-AUC cards 0.197 [0.179, 0.214], 0.593 [0.564, 0.618], and 0.242 [0.215, 0.273] without premises; 2014 and 2023 shares 20.0% and 10.5% ("a fifth", "about a tenth" are fair).
- **Explain.** Model-based post-2019 interval 0.53 to 0.56; 15:00 OR 2.36 [2.12, 2.63]; dark 1.14 [1.06, 1.22]; 28 of 41 time terms (hour 21 of 23, month 5 of 11, weekday 1 of 6, dark 1 of 1, all by robust interval; the 41 excludes post-2019); AIC +517; ROC-AUC 0.631, sd 0.003, 5 folds; 158 neighbourhoods; 159,056 rows. The 3 and 7 rises in hourly counts were recomputed from `incidents_by_hour`. Summer 14.1% vs winter 20.2%; Fri and Sat 443; Sun 332 fewest robberies; Wed the most incidents (2,609 vs Fri 2,587, a tiny margin); 16.4% and 17.2%. Calibration deciles are out-of-fold. The risk and causation caveats are present.
- **Predict.** Every cell of the models table, the "adds" table, baselines 16.1% and 11.0%, ROC 0.836 to 0.599, the 20.0% to 10.5% shift, 158 clusters, 1,000 replicates. The benchmark table matches `jsc370_benchmark`; the tier ranking is identical; 0.577 vs 0.363 and the 40-row grid are right; 108 vs 40 is on the results side. The F1 threshold optimism is disclosed in Methods (`evaluation.threshold`).
- **Methods.** 174,993 / 332 / 350 / 159,072 / 16 / 159,056; 25,657 robberies (16.1%); seed 303313; 43 parameters. The task definition is correct and says "not the risk of robbery at a place or time".
- **Paper.** `numbers.tex` and both generated tables match the JSON for every cell I opened (ladder A and B, the `\res` values, deltas); the abstract and Results values are right as quantities (0.55 [0.52, 0.58]; 2,893 → 2,347; 5,082 → 10,842; 15:00 = 2.36 and 08:00 = 0.48 are the highest and lowest hours; Dec = 0.83 is the largest month effect; 0.334 / 0.328 / 0.012 / 0.004 / 0.050 and the L5−L4 interval −0.005 to 0.005 on Design A; B L1 0.141, L5 0.435, ROC 0.841; importance 0.071 / 0.414 / 0.022 / 0.021; 21 of 23 hours). The Discussion limits (composition, reporting, premises definitional, census coarse, optimistic F1) are present and honest.
- **Excluded or cut material.** The paper does not use Explorer numbers or the PR curves (the Paper page says so). The six-model benchmark appears only as the qualitative "corrections" paragraph. The page budget is 6 pages. No "will", "predicts where/when" or safety wording found in the product copy. "Risk" appears only in negations.
- **Bibliography.** Recognised as real publications with the stated details: Shmueli 2010 (Stat Sci 25(3) 289-310); Breiman 2001 two cultures (16(3) 199-231); Breiman 2001 Random Forests (Mach Learn 45(1) 5-32); Chen & Guestrin 2016 (KDD, 785-794); Lum & Isaac 2016 (Significance 13(5) 14-19); Cameron & Miller 2015 (JHR 50(2) 317-372); Roberts et al. 2017 (Ecography 40(8) 913-929); Saito & Rehmsmeier 2015 (PLOS ONE 10(3) e0118432); Efron & Tibshirani 1993 (Chapman & Hall); Satyanarayan et al. 2017 (IEEE TVCG 23(1) 341-350).

## Could not verify

- **UNVERIFIED: `skogan1977dark`** (Skogan 1977, "Dimensions of the Dark Figure of Unreported Crime", Crime & Delinquency 23(1), 41-50). I recognise the author and title as real, but a web search did not confirm the journal, volume and pages. It only found an NCJRS abstract of a same-titled Skogan paper dated 1975 (15 pages).
